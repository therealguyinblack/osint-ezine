### AI: AI Tools in Linux Intrusions.

SANS examines how Linux incident response is changing as attackers combine software supply-chain compromise, eBPF rootkits, exposed self-hosted LLMs and agentic AI tooling. The article is useful for investigators because it focuses on where evidence actually lives when standard host tools can no longer be fully trusted.

[https://www.sans.org/blog/investigating-ai-tools-modern-linux-intrusions](https://www.sans.org/blog/investigating-ai-tools-modern-linux-intrusions)

### AI: Claude Fable 5 Jailbreak Claims.

Cybersecurity News reports claims that Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5 was jailbroken shortly after launch, allegedly allowing restricted cybersecurity outputs and exposing parts of the model’s system prompt. The report is relevant to AI safety, red teaming and defensive security research, although the claims should be treated as allegations unless further confirmed by Anthropic or independent researchers.

[https://cybersecuritynews.com/anthropics-claude-fable-5-jailbroken/](https://cybersecuritynews.com/anthropics-claude-fable-5-jailbroken/)

### Learning: AI Triage in OSINT.

OSINT Combine explains how generative AI can support triage across large datasets without replacing analyst judgement. ### News: Patch the Planet.

Trail of Bits introduced Patch the Planet, an initiative using frontier AI models and security engineers to find, triage and fix bugs in open-source projects. The first week produced dozens of pull requests and issues across critical projects, highlighting that AI can increase discovery volume but human validation, patching and disclosure remain essential.

[https://blog.trailofbits.com/2026/06/22/introducing-patch-the-planet/](https://blog.trailofbits.com/2026/06/22/introducing-patch-the-planet/)

### News: Post-Quantum Cryptography Executive Order.

The White House issued Executive Order 14412 on securing the United States against advanced cryptographic attacks. It directs federal agencies to accelerate migration to NIST-approved post-quantum cryptography, with deadlines for high-value and high-impact systems, reflecting the growing urgency around “harvest now, decrypt later” threats.

[https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/securing-the-nation-against-advanced-cryptographic-attacks/](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/securing-the-nation-against-advanced-cryptographic-attacks/)

### Privacy: Identity Documents Exposed Online.

This article reports that nearly a million passports and photo IDs were exposed through public URLs linked to identity verification systems used by cannabis clubs and related services. The incident is a reminder that identity documents are not like passwords, and once exposed they can create long-term fraud and account takeover risks.

[https://cambridgeanalytica.org/data-breaches-scandals/passports-driver-licenses-exposed-public-internet-2026-51096/](https://cambridgeanalytica.org/data-breaches-scandals/passports-driver-licenses-exposed-public-internet-2026-51096/)

### Privacy: Instagram Meta AI Account Recovery Flaw.

Cybersecurity News reports an alleged Instagram account recovery flaw where Meta AI could be manipulated to request password reset emails for some users. Meta stated there was no breach of its systems, but the case shows how AI-assisted support functions can become sensitive attack surfaces when linked to account recovery.

[https://cybersecuritynews.com/instagram-meta-ai-vulnerability/](https://cybersecuritynews.com/instagram-meta-ai-vulnerability/)

### Privacy: System-Level Anonymity.

Threema explains why usernames alone are not enough for private messaging if a service still requires a phone number. The post distinguishes between anonymity from chat partners and anonymity from the service provider, arguing that privacy must be built into the system architecture rather than added as a user-facing feature.

[https://threema.com/en/blog/anonymity-at-the-system-level-the-ultimate-privacy-protection](https://threema.com/en/blog/anonymity-at-the-system-level-the-ultimate-privacy-protection)

### Tools: XORCISM.

XORCISM is an open-source cybersecurity platform for cyber exposure management, threat intelligence, vulnerability management, compliance, OSINT, incident response and attack-surface analysis. It includes more than 1,200 connectors and combines frameworks such as ATT\&CK, D3FEND, STIX, TAXII and AI threat modelling modules.
