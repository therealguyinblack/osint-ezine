### AI: Living With OpenClaw

I spent 30 days living with an AI agent. It was a very interesting experiment and this article explores what heavy everyday use of an open-source AI agent can look like in practice, from news monitoring to task automation across messaging platforms. The key takeaway is not only productivity, but risk: poorly secured agent deployments can become highly privileged attack surfaces on personal systems.

[https://therealguyinblack.gitbook.io/osint-ezine/articles/artificial-intelligence-and-defence/202603\_01\_30-days-living-with-openclaw](https://therealguyinblack.gitbook.io/osint-ezine/articles/artificial-intelligence-and-defence/202603_01_30-days-living-with-openclaw)

### Tools: Netryx Geolocation Engine

Netryx is a local-first geolocation tool that identifies the exact GPS coordinates of any street-level photograph. Unlike reverse image search (which matches against uploaded web images), Netryx matches against systematically crawled street-view panoramas, meaning it works on any random street corner with zero internet presence.

[https://github.com/sparkyniner/Netryx-OpenSource-Next-Gen-Street-Level-Geolocation](https://github.com/sparkyniner/Netryx-OpenSource-Next-Gen-Street-Level-Geolocation)
