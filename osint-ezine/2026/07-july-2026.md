### AI: AI Defense Matrix

The AI Defense Matrix is a free framework designed to help organisations identify gaps, assign responsibilities and select controls for protecting AI systems. It maps AI-specific asset classes against the functions of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 and distinguishes between using AI for security and securing AI itself.

[https://aidefensematrix.com/](https://aidefensematrix.com/)

### AI: AI Lowers Barriers to Extremist Capability

This GNET analysis examines how advanced AI systems may reduce the technical expertise required by violent extremists to develop harmful capabilities. It focuses on the potential for models to support planning, troubleshooting and technical learning, particularly in relation to chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear and explosive threats, while avoiding operational instructions.

[https://gnet-research.org/2026/07/27/artificial-intelligence-and-extremist-capability-how-ai-lowers-the-barriers-between-intent-and-capability/](https://gnet-research.org/2026/07/27/artificial-intelligence-and-extremist-capability-how-ai-lowers-the-barriers-between-intent-and-capability/)

### News: Canadian NATO Intern Arrested for Suspected Espionage

A Canadian intern working at NATO headquarters was reportedly arrested in Belgium on suspicion of spying for an unidentified foreign power. Public information remains limited, and the allegations had not been proven at the time of publication, but the case raises questions about personnel vetting, insider threats and access controls within international organisations.

[https://covertaccessteam.substack.com/p/canadian-nato-intern-arrested-in](https://covertaccessteam.substack.com/p/canadian-nato-intern-arrested-in)

### News: Google Introduces a Unified Threat Actor Naming System

Google Threat Intelligence Group has introduced a unified naming system to replace the separate taxonomies previously used by Mandiant and Google Threat Analysis Group. The system combines a unique first word with a second word indicating the suspected origin, motivation or activity type, helping defenders understand attribution context without memorising unrelated identifiers.

[https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/updated-cyber-threat-actor-naming-system](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/updated-cyber-threat-actor-naming-system)

### Privacy: ChatGPT and Roblox Face Strict EU Platform Rules

The European Commission is reportedly considering designating ChatGPT and Roblox as very large online platforms under the Digital Services Act. Such a designation would apply enhanced transparency, risk assessment, auditing and regulatory fee requirements to services exceeding 45 million monthly users in the European Union.

[https://reclaimthenet.org/chatgpt-and-roblox-face-the-eus-strictest-online-rules](https://reclaimthenet.org/chatgpt-and-roblox-face-the-eus-strictest-online-rules)

### Techniques: Discord ID Investigations

Discord Snowflake IDs are permanent numerical identifiers assigned to users, messages, servers and channels. This guide explains how investigators can obtain and decode them to establish creation timestamps, preserve stable references and support cross-platform correlation, while emphasising that an identifier is an investigative pivot rather than proof of identity.

[https://www.osint.industries/post/discord-id-lookup-how-to-find-use-and-investigate-discord-ids-with-osint](https://www.osint.industries/post/discord-id-lookup-how-to-find-use-and-investigate-discord-ids-with-osint)

### Techniques: Google Dorking in the AI Search Era

OSINT Combine examines whether the expansion of AI-generated search features has reduced the effectiveness of traditional Google dorking. The assessment concludes that advanced search operators remain useful, although changes to result presentation and the increasing prominence of AI-generated answers may affect investigative workflows and make source verification more important.

[https://www.osintcombine.com/post/has-googles-obsession-with-ai-ruined-google-dorking](https://www.osintcombine.com/post/has-googles-obsession-with-ai-ruined-google-dorking)

### Techniques: Kinahan Cartel Lieutenant Located in Dubai

Bellingcat used social media material, sports imagery and other open sources to locate Ian Dixon, a person sanctioned by the United States for his alleged role in the Kinahan Organised Crime Group, at padel events in Dubai. Dixon has no known criminal convictions, but US authorities allege that he managed finances, moved bulk currency and monitored debts for members of the organisation.

[https://www.bellingcat.com/news/2026/06/27/poster-boy-sanctioned-kinahan-cartel-lieutenant-found-playing-padel-in-dubai/](https://www.bellingcat.com/news/2026/06/27/poster-boy-sanctioned-kinahan-cartel-lieutenant-found-playing-padel-in-dubai/)

### Techniques: Social Media Profile Discovery

This five-step methodology covers the discovery and verification of public social media profiles from a seed identifier such as a name, username, email address, telephone number or photograph. It combines identifier expansion, username enumeration, cross-platform correlation, metadata review and historical recovery while stressing the need to distinguish verified accounts from false matches.

[https://shadowdragon.io/resources/osint-social-media-search/](https://shadowdragon.io/resources/osint-social-media-search/)

### Tools: WayTrace

WayTrace is an open-source reconnaissance tool that queries information already archived by the Internet Archive Wayback Machine. It can help investigators explore historical URLs and changes without actively scanning or sending traffic to the target website, reducing operational exposure during preliminary research.

[https://github.com/thomashousset/WayTrace](https://github.com/thomashousset/WayTrace)
